Our Story

Founded in 2022 by a group of Nepali footballers in Irving, Texas — built on the belief that a ball at your feet can hold an entire community together.

Where We Come From

From Weekend Pickup Games To A Registered Club

Irving Nepal FC began as informal weekend games among Nepali families in the DFW metroplex, looking for a way to stay connected to home. What started with a handful of players on a borrowed field grew into a fully registered club within a year.

Today we field competitive men's sides, run a growing youth academy, and serve as a gathering point for the wider Nepali diaspora in North Texas — on matchdays and well beyond them.

Every crest detail carries meaning: the pagoda skyline of the Kathmandu Valley, the red and blue of the Nepali flag, and a player in full stride — reaching for something bigger than the scoreline.

Mission

Unity Through Football

Build a home away from home for the Nepali community of Irving through the game we all grew up loving.

Vision

Compete & Grow

Field competitive sides at the highest amateur level while growing a youth pipeline for the next generation.

Values

Community First

Every match is also a reunion — for players, families, and supporters who call Irving home.

Culture

Proudly Nepali

We carry the colors, the pride, and the spirit of Nepal onto every pitch we step on in Texas.

Good To Know

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join Irving Nepal FC?

+

Head to the Join page, choose a membership plan, and submit the registration form. A club rep will follow up with next steps and payment.

What ages can join the Youth Academy?

+

The academy welcomes players ages 6 to 16, split into age-appropriate training groups coached by current first-team players.

Do I need to be Nepali to play?

+

Not at all. INFC was founded by the Nepali community of Irving, but the club welcomes any player who shares our love of the game.

How can my business become a sponsor?

+

Visit the Contact page and select "Sponsorship" as your subject. We offer kit, matchday, and academy sponsorship packages.

Where does the club play home matches?

+

Most home fixtures are played at MacArthur Park in Irving, with occasional matches at Cimarron Park and Lively Pointe.
